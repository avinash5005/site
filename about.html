Our Story

Why we started H&S WebCraft

Many local shops and growing businesses still don't have a professional website, or they have one that is slow, outdated, and doesn't bring in customers. We started H&S WebCraft to fix exactly that.

We are a small team from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, and each of us owns one part of the job: talking to clients, designing, building the front end and back end, and setting up secure payments. Because everyone does what they are best at, your website is faster to deliver and better in quality.
